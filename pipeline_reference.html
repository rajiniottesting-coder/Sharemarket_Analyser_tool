Part 4 — Composite assembly & verdict derivation

Part 3 ended with five sub-scores. Part 4 picks up there and runs the rest of the way to the verdict you see in the Excel. The blended sub-score gets five composite-level adjustments (MoS · Spike · Early Mover · Anti-trigger · Forensic — together a ±25-point swing) and is then put through a five-gate verdict-derivation flow (v10.17 added the data-completeness gate). Part 6 — Outcome tracking v17.19

Every Gold pick is logged once and walked forward on every run. The tracker is invoked by the pipeline itself (since v14.1.3) before the Excel is built, so today's picks and fresh P&L appear in today's workbook.

Quick-pick labels are separate from the verdict

stock["verdict"] (BUY / WATCHLIST / NEUTRAL / OVERVALUED / AVOID) and stock["label"] (DEEP VALUE / EARLY MOVER / …) come from different functions. The verdict drives colour and the Gold filter; the label is an archetype used for sorting and for the Performance breakdowns.
