── 3A. VALUATION RATIOS ─────────────────────────────

P/E Ratio (TTM + 1yr forward):
  vs sector median AND stock's own 5yr avg P/E
  Current P/E < Own 5yr Avg × 0.85 → "HIST. CHEAP" tag

Earnings Yield = EPS / CMP × 100
  > 6%  → Beats 10Y Gsec → ATTRACTIVE (green)
  4–6%  → Moderate
  < 4%  → Low yield (justify only with high growth)
  Show: "P/E 28x = Earnings Yield 3.6%"

P/B Ratio:
  vs book value 12-month trend + sector 5yr median P/B

EV/EBITDA:
  > 20x → Flag EXPENSIVE (amber)
  Use sector peer median for comparison

Price/Sales (P/S Ratio):
  Useful for pre-profit high-growth companies

PEG Ratio = P/E ÷ EPS Growth Rate (3yr forward CAGR)
  Cap growth at 30%
  < 0.5   → "DEEPLY UNDERVALUED FOR GROWTH" (green)
  0.5–1.0 → "UNDERVALUED" (green)
  1.0–1.5 → "FAIRLY VALUED" (neutral)
  1.5–2.0 → "SLIGHTLY EXPENSIVE" (amber)
  > 2.0   → "EXPENSIVE FOR GROWTH RATE" (red)

Price-to-Cash Flow (P/CF) = CMP / (Operating CF/Share)
  < 15  → Attractive (green)
  > 30  → Rich; verify CF quality (amber)
  RED FLAG: PAT growing but CFO flat/declining 2Q+
  → Auto-tag "CFO DIVERGENCE" risk signal

Graham Number = √(22.5 × EPS_TTM × Book Value/Share)
  Skip: negative EPS or pure financial company

── 3J. SMART MONEY TRACKER (NEW IN v4.0) ────────────

OBJECTIVE: Track WHERE informed money is moving BEFORE
price reacts. Update daily from NSE + BSE data feeds.

Track and alert on:
  A) BULK DEAL PATTERNS
     Same institution buying same stock 3+ times in 90D
     → "REPEAT BUYER" signal (very high conviction)
     Use NSE bulk deal + BSE bulk deal data

  B) FII ACTIVITY IN LESS-COVERED STOCKS
     FII buying a stock that has NO existing FII holding
     → "FII INITIATION" signal (first foreign interest)

  C) MUTUAL FUND NEW POSITION
     Any AMFI-listed MF appears as NEW holder in a stock
     (not in last quarter's holdings)
     → "MF NEW ENTRY" signal

  D) DII CONTRA BUY
     DII buying while FII selling in same stock for 2Q+
     → "DII CONVICTION" signal (domestic value buying)

  E) BLOCK DEAL AT PREMIUM
     Block deal executed at > 2% premium to CMP
     → "PREMIUM BLOCK" signal (buyer paying up = bullish)

  F) PROMOTER OPEN MARKET PURCHASE
     Promoter buying in open market (not ESOP, not rights)
     → "PROMOTER BUYING" signal (highest insider signal)
     Distinguish from: pledged share release, ESOP exercise

SMART MONEY TAGS (show in Block C signals):
  [REPEAT BUYER] [FII INITIATION] [MF NEW ENTRY]
  [DII CONVICTION] [PREMIUM BLOCK] [PROMOTER BUYING]

SECTION 5B — FAIR VALUE ENGINE
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Run BEFORE Section 6. CFV must inform verdict score.

STEP 1 — 7 VALUATION MODELS:

M1. DCF — 3-Stage Discounted Cash Flow
  WACC = 10Y Gsec yield + Beta × ERP (5.5%)
  Stage 1 (Yr 1–5): FCF growth, capped at 25%
  Stage 2 (Yr 6–10): Taper to sector median growth
  Stage 3: Terminal Value = FCF(Yr10)×(1+g)/(WACC−g)
  Terminal g = 4.5% (India nominal GDP)

M2. Graham Number = √(22.5 × EPS_TTM × BV/Share)
  Skip: negative EPS or pure financial company

M3. P/E Mean Reversion
  PE_FV = EPS_TTM × Median_Sector_PE (5yr historical)
  Quality premium: +10–25% for franchise businesses

M4. P/B Mean Reversion
  PB_FV = BV/Share × Median_Sector_PB (5yr historical)
  Banks: use Adjusted Book (net of NPAs)

M5. EV/EBITDA Peer Comparable
  EV_FV = (Sector Median EV/EBITDA × EBITDA) − Net Debt
  Use 3–5 listed peers, same sector + size band

M6. Dividend Discount Model (DDM)
  DDM_FV = D1 / (Ke − g)
  Apply ONLY: Div Yield > 1% + consistent payout 5yr+

M7. PEG-Adjusted Fair Value
  PEG_FV = EPS_TTM × EPS_Growth_Rate_%
  Cap growth at 30%; use 3yr forward EPS CAGR

STEP 2 — COMPOSITE FAIR VALUE (CFV) BY SECTOR:

IT / Software:
  DCF 35% | PE 30% | PEG 20% | EV/EBITDA 10% | G 5%

Banks / NBFCs:
  PB 35% | PE 25% | DDM 20% | DCF 15% | EV/EBITDA 5%

FMCG / Consumer:
  PE 30% | DCF 30% | DDM 20% | PEG 10% | EV/EBITDA 10%

Pharma / Healthcare:
  DCF 35% | PE 25% | EV/EBITDA 20% | PEG 15% | G 5%

Infrastructure / Capital Goods:
  EV/EBITDA 35% | DCF 30% | PE 20% | PB 10% | G 5%

Metals / Commodities:
  EV/EBITDA 40% | PB 25% | PE 20% | DCF 10% | G 5%

Diversified / Conglomerates:
  DCF 30% | EV/EBITDA 25% | PE 20% | PB 15% | G 10%

BSE SME / Micro Cap:
  PE 35% | PEG 30% | DCF 25% | G 10%
  (Skip EV/EBITDA, PB, DDM for SME — unreliable)

CFV = Σ (Model_FV × Model_Weight)
CFV_Low  = CFV × 0.85
CFV_High = CFV × 1.15

STEP 3 — MARGIN OF SAFETY:
  MoS % = (CFV − CMP) / CFV × 100
  Upside_to_FV = (CFV − CMP) / CMP × 100

  > 40%         → EXCEPTIONAL — Rare deep value
  25–40%        → STRONG — Accumulate
  10–25%        → ADEQUATE — Buy in tranches
  0–10%         → THIN — Wait for dip
  −1% to −15%   → SLIGHT PREMIUM — Caution
  < −15%        → SIGNIFICANT PREMIUM — Avoid/exit

STEP 4 — CFV Score Adjustment:
  MoS > 40%    → +10 | 25–40% → +6 | 10–25% → +3
  0–10%        →  0  | −1–−15% → −4 | < −15% → −8

STEP 5 — Sensitivity Table (on request):
  DCF: WACC 9%/10%/11% × Bear/Base/Bull FCF growth
  PE:  P/E 18x/22x/26x × Bear/Base/Bull EPS

12B — GATE CHECK (19:00 IST — SINGLE CONSOLIDATED CHECK)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Run this single check at 19:00 IST every calendar day.
All conditions must pass (ALL green) before ANY other
process starts. If ANY condition fails → SKIP today.

The check runs as one Python function: gate_check()
Returns: {"run": True/False, "reason": "...", "log": [...]}

CONDITION 1 — WEEKEND CHECK:
  Logic  : from datetime import date; d = date.today()
           d.weekday() in [5, 6]  # 5=Saturday, 6=Sunday
  FAIL   : Today is Saturday or Sunday
  Message: "SKIP — Weekend. No markets open."
  Action : Log + halt. No retries. Resume Monday 19:00.

CONDITION 2 — MARKET HOLIDAY CHECK:
  Logic  : Query local DB:
           SELECT COUNT(*) FROM market_holidays
           WHERE date = date('now','localtime')
           AND exchange = 'NSE'
  FAIL   : Count > 0 (today is a listed NSE holiday)
  Message: "SKIP — Market holiday: [holiday_name]"
  Action : Log + halt. Resume next trading day.
  Fallback: If DB query fails, check hardcoded dict
            HOLIDAYS_2026 in config.py (see 12C below)

CONDITION 3 — NSE BHAV COPY FILE AVAILABILITY:
  Logic  : Build today's NSE URL:
           from datetime import date
           d = date.today()
           fname = f"BhavCopy_NSE_CM_0_0_0_{d:%Y%m%d}_F_0000.csv.zip"
           url = f"https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/cm/{fname}"
           Perform HTTP HEAD request (not GET — fast, no download)
           Check: response.status_code == 200
  FAIL   : HTTP status != 200 (file not yet published or URL error)
  Message: "SKIP — NSE Bhav file not available. HTTP [status]."
  Retry  : Wait 15 min → retry once at 19:15 IST.
           If still fails → SKIP today. Log + halt.
  Note   : HEAD request only — do not download at this step.

CONDITION 4 — BSE BHAV COPY FILE AVAILABILITY:
  Logic  : Build today's BSE URL:
           d = date.today()
           fname = f"EQ{d:%d%m%y}_CSV.ZIP"
           url = f"https://www.bseindia.com/download/BhavCopy/Equity/{fname}"
           Perform HTTP HEAD request
           Check: response.status_code == 200
  FAIL   : HTTP status != 200
  Message: "SKIP — BSE Bhav file not available. HTTP [status]."
  Retry  : Same 15-min retry logic as Condition 3.
  Note   : If NSE passes but BSE fails after retry →
           proceed with NSE-only mode, flag BSE as unavailable.
           Mark all BSE-only stocks as "Data unavailable today".

CONDITION 5 — DATA INTEGRITY CHECK (post-download, 19:10):
  Runs immediately after both files are downloaded.
  Logic  : Load NSE CSV · count rows · check column names
           NSE: must have columns SYMBOL, OPEN, HIGH, LOW,
                CLOSE, TOTTRDQTY, TOTTRDVAL
                AND row count > 1000 (sanity: NSE lists ~2000+)
           BSE: must have CODE, OPEN, HIGH, LOW, CLOSE
                AND row count > 500
  FAIL   : Missing columns OR row count below threshold
  Message: "SKIP — Data integrity failure: [exchange] file
           has [N] rows / missing columns [list]."
  Action : Log + halt entire pipeline. Do NOT proceed with
           corrupt or incomplete data.

CONDITION 6 — MINIMUM WATCHLIST CHECK:
  Logic  : SELECT COUNT(*) FROM watchlist WHERE active = 1
  FAIL   : Count = 0 (no stocks configured to analyse)
  Message: "SKIP — Watchlist is empty. Add stocks to watchlist."
  Action : Log + halt. Send alert notification to user.

GATE CHECK RESULT LOGGING:
  Write to run_log table:
  run_date | check_time | condition | status | message
  All 6 conditions logged regardless of pass/fail.
  If ALL pass → run_log entry: status='RUN_APPROVED'
  If ANY fail → run_log entry: status='PIPELINE_SKIPPED'

GATE CHECK PYTHON SKELETON:
  def gate_check() -> dict:
      ist = pytz.timezone('Asia/Kolkata')
      today = datetime.now(ist).date()
      log = []

      # C1: Weekend
      if today.weekday() in [5, 6]:
          return {"run": False, "reason": "Weekend", "log": log}

      # C2: Holiday
      if is_market_holiday(today):   # queries DB + config fallback
          name = get_holiday_name(today)
          return {"run": False, "reason": f"Holiday: {name}", "log": log}

      # C3: NSE file
      nse_url = build_nse_bhav_url(today)
      if not http_head_ok(nse_url):
          time.sleep(900)            # wait 15 min
          if not http_head_ok(nse_url):
              return {"run": False, "reason": "NSE file unavailable", "log": log}

      # C4: BSE file
      bse_url = build_bse_bhav_url(today)
      bse_ok = http_head_ok(bse_url)
      if not bse_ok:
          time.sleep(900)
          bse_ok = http_head_ok(bse_url)
      # Note: BSE failure alone doesn't halt — runs NSE-only mode

      # C5 + C6 run AFTER download at 19:10
      # (integrity + watchlist checked before Stage 1)

      return {"run": True, "bse_available": bse_ok, "log": log}

SECTION 0 — PRE-SCREENING ENGINE (Filter & Drill-Down)
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PURPOSE:
  Reduce the 5,000+ NSE+BSE universe to exactly 100 stocks
  for Claude expert analysis each day. All three stages run
  in Python — zero API cost. Only the final 100 stocks
  consume Claude tokens via the Anthropic API.

  This runs AFTER gate_check passes (Section 12B) and AFTER
  Bhav Copy data is loaded into the DB (19:15–19:30 IST).

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
0A — STAGE 1: VOLUME / LIQUIDITY / PRICE QUALITY FILTER
     Input: All stocks in DB for today | Output: ~400–600
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Run entirely from today's Bhav Copy data + historical DB.
No fundamental data needed. Pure price/volume math.

KEEP a stock if ALL of the following are true:
  V1  Volume today > 0 (stock actually traded)
  V2  Volume today ≥ 1.5× its own 20-day average volume
      OR stock is in user's personal watchlist
  V3  Delivery % ≥ 40%  (not purely intraday speculative)
  V4  CMP ≥ ₹2  (exclude penny/shell stocks)
  V5  Market cap ≥ ₹50Cr  (minimum liquidity floor)
  V6  CMP within 40% below 52W high  (not already crashed out)
      OR CMP within 20% above 52W low  (potential reversal)
      OR stock in personal watchlist (override)
  V7  Not a circuit-hit stock (abs price change < 19.9%)
      UNLESS also in personal watchlist
  V8  Not suspended / under surveillance (check DB flag)
  V9  BSE SME stocks must have avg daily turnover ≥ ₹5L

DROP rules (hard exclusions):
  D1  Zero volume today → drop (not traded)
  D2  Flagged SEBI suspension in stock_master table → drop
  D3  Price change > +19.9% without proportional volume
      (circuit hit by operator manipulation pattern) → drop
  D4  Last full Claude analysis < 3 days ago AND no new
      BSE/NSE corporate announcement today → skip today
      (avoid redundant re-analysis of unchanged stocks)

WATCHLIST OVERRIDE: Any stock in user's watchlist table
  (watchlist WHERE active=1) passes Stage 1 unconditionally.
  Watchlist stocks are ALWAYS analysed regardless of volume.

OUTPUT: List of symbols → pass to Stage 2.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
0B — STAGE 2: LIGHTWEIGHT FUNDAMENTAL SCORER
     Input: Stage 1 output | Output: ~150–200
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Reads stored fundamental data from DB (loaded from
Screener.in / Tickertape API / manual quarterly upload).
Scores 0–30 using 6 fast binary criteria.
Drops stocks scoring < 10/30.

SCORING CRITERIA (each scores 5 points):
  F1  +5  PAT positive in at least 2 of last 4 quarters
          (not a chronic loss-making company)
  F2  +5  Revenue YoY growth > 0%
          (at minimum not shrinking)
  F3  +5  Debt/Equity < 1.5
          (not dangerously over-leveraged)
  F4  +5  Promoter holding > 25%
          (minimum insider alignment)
  F5  +5  P/E ratio < 80x OR P/E not applicable
          (not grotesquely overvalued on earnings basis)
  F6  +5  No active SEBI alert / fraud flag in DB
          AND no exchange suspension / watchlist flag

SCORE INTERPRETATION:
  25–30  : Strong fundamentals → high priority for Stage 3
  15–24  : Acceptable → eligible for Stage 3
  10–14  : Borderline → only if volume signal very strong
  0–9    : Fail → DROP (do not pass to Stage 3)

HARD DROP RULES (override score, instant elimination):
  HD1  Promoter holding = 0% AND no institutional holding
       → DROP regardless of score (governance black hole)
  HD2  3+ consecutive quarterly PAT losses → DROP
  HD3  Promoter pledge > 40% → DROP
       (extreme margin call risk, not worth deep analysis)
  HD4  Company has < 2 years of financial history in DB
       → DROP (insufficient data for meaningful analysis)

WATCHLIST OVERRIDE: Watchlist stocks pass Stage 2
  unconditionally regardless of fundamental score.
  They receive a note: "Watchlist override — analyse regardless."

OUTPUT: Symbols with Stage 2 scores → pass to Stage 3.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
0C — STAGE 3: PRIORITY RANKER (selects top 100 for Claude)
     Input: Stage 2 output | Output: exactly 100 symbols
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Compute a Priority Score (0–100) for each surviving stock.
Sort descending. Take top 100. Apply override rules.

PRIORITY SCORE FORMULA:
  P = (vol_spike_ratio × 40)
    + (stage2_score / 30 × 25)
    + (delivery_pct / 100 × 20)
    + (recency_bonus × 15)

  Where:
    vol_spike_ratio  = min(today_vol / avg_20d_vol, 10) / 10
                       (capped at 10× so extreme spikes don't dominate)
    stage2_score     = 0–30 from Stage 2
    delivery_pct     = today's delivery % from Bhav Copy
    recency_bonus    = 1.0 if not analysed in last 7 days
                       0.7 if analysed 4–6 days ago
                       0.3 if analysed 2–3 days ago
                       0.0 if analysed within 48 hours

OVERRIDE RULES (force into top 100 regardless of rank):
  O1  WATCHLIST OVERRIDE: All stocks in active watchlist
      → always included. Count against the 100 cap.
  O2  BSE/NSE ANNOUNCEMENT TODAY: Any stock with a new
      regulatory filing / corporate announcement / board
      meeting outcome dated today → force include.
  O3  SPIKE PRE-TRIGGER: Any stock where Stage 1+2 data
      already satisfies 2+ spike trigger conditions
      (volume > 3×, delivery > 60%, VWAP above) → include.
  O4  SCORE DETERIORATION: Any stock whose last Claude
      composite score was ≥ 60 but today's Stage 2 score
      suggests deterioration (< 15) → include to check
      whether an exit alert is needed.
  O5  EXPIRY RULE: Any stock not analysed in 7+ days
      regardless of today's signal → include once a week.

CAP MANAGEMENT:
  If overrides cause total > 100:
    Overrides fill slots first (O1 priority > O2 > O3 > O4 > O5)
    Remaining slots filled by ranked Priority Score
  If Stage 2 survivors < 100:
    Use all available stocks (Claude analyses fewer that day)
    Log: "Only [N] stocks passed filters today"

PRIORITISED ORDERING WITHIN 100:
  1. Personal watchlist (sorted by last analysis age DESC)
  2. Override O2: New announcement today
  3. Override O3: Spike pre-trigger
  4. Override O4: Deterioration check
  5. Ranked by Priority Score (remaining slots)
  6. Override O5: Expiry fills last

OUTPUT: Ordered list of 100 symbols → Claude API queue.
  Also output: stage1_count, stage2_count, overrides_applied
  Store in run_log table for monitoring.

0D — CLAUDE ANALYSIS BATCH EXECUTION

After Stage 3 outputs the 100 symbols:

BATCH STRATEGY:
  Process stocks in batches of 10–15 per API call

    - The full master prompt v7.0 as system prompt
    - Today's Bhav Copy data for those 10–15 stocks

TIMEOUT HANDLING:

  If retry fails → skip that batch → log skipped symbols.

RATE LIMITING:
